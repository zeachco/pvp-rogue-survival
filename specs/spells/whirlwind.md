# Whirlwind (`whirlwind`)

**Source:** extractable from Rare and Epic axes. **Activation:** automatic 3-rage skill with an 18-second base cooldown. **Effect:** for `3 + 9 * (skillLevel - 1) / 98` seconds (3 seconds at level 1, 12 seconds at level 99), spinning physical weapon edges follow the hero, pulse every 0.25 seconds for `1 + 0.4 * Strength` damage, and use radius `1.8 + 0.024 * skillLevel` m. While active, its movement-speed multiplier scales linearly as `0.5 + (skillLevel - 1) / 98`, from half speed at level 1 to 1.5× speed at level 99; this effect is exclusive to Whirlwind and is not an item slow. The persistent visual follows the hero at the same radius and duration. It renders overlapping shader-displaced 3D circular ribbons at elbow height spinning continuously into a soft, nearly unbroken motion-blurred trail, with no discrete rotating blade or cone.
