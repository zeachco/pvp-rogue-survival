# Sunburn (`sunburnAura`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** every nearby enemy takes Fire damage equal to `1% + 0.09% * Intelligence` of its maximum HP, capped at 10%, every `max(0.5, 5 - 0.045 * Spirit)` seconds. The ground effect uses orange flares.
