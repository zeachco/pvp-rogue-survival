# Penance (`penance`)

**Source:** extractable Idol perk passive. **Activation:** togglable passive with Rage upkeep of `0.002 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** after a successful buckler block, restores Mana equal to `max(blocked damage * Spirit * level conversion, 1% maximum Mana)`, capped at maximum Mana. Level conversion scales linearly from 1% at level 1 to 30% at level 99. The tooltip states this trigger, formula, and minimum return directly and shows the current and next level-conversion percentages.
