# Penance (`penance`)

**Source:** extractable Idol perk passive. **Activation:** zero-cost togglable passive. **Effect:** after a successful buckler block, restores Mana equal to `blocked damage * Spirit * level conversion`, capped at maximum Mana. Level conversion scales linearly from 1% at level 1 to 60% at level 99. The tooltip states this trigger and formula directly and shows the current and next level-conversion percentages.
