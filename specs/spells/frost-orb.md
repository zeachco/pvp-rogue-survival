# Frozen Orb (`frostOrb`)

**Source:** Rare and Epic staves. **Activation:** automatic 10-mana magic skill; 20-second base cooldown and 10 m base range. **Effect:** an orb represented by two minimally extruded five-point low-poly stars in slightly different blues rotates those stars in opposite directions at different speeds while traveling at 1.5 m/s. It lives 4 seconds and emits eight radial, single-hit five-sided 3D cones every 0.45 seconds. Each cone points along its world-space flight direction and neither the orb nor its spikes billboard toward the camera. Orb and spikes apply a 4-second Freeze. Freeze cancels self-propelled velocity and steering, but later Force Field velocity slides without friction until it ends.
