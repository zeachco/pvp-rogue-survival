# Frozen Orb (`frostOrb`)

**Source:** Rare and Epic staves. **Activation:** automatic 10-mana magic skill; 20-second base cooldown and 10 m base range. **Effect:** a 1.5 m/s orb lives 4 seconds and emits eight radial, single-hit spikes every 0.45 seconds. Orb and spikes apply a 4-second Freeze. Freeze cancels self-propelled velocity and steering, but later Force Field velocity slides without friction until it ends.
