# Healing (`healing`)

**Source:** Rare, Epic, and Unique maces can supply or upgrade it. Sent creeps may gain it from an equipped item or authored universal skill. **Activation:** hero and creep auto-fire waits until current HP is at or below 50% of maximum HP; pressing its assigned shortcut may cast it manually at any missing-HP value. **Effect:** restores its level-scaled 40%–100% fraction of current HP, plus a flat 5% of maximum HP and a further 5% of maximum HP scaled by the caster's current Rage fraction (`currentRage / maxRage`); restoration is capped by missing HP. Each cast costs `5 + 2 * effective skill level + 0.25 * HP actually restored` mana. For a creep's area cast, the variable component is based on HP restored to the caster; healing nearby allies does not add to its cost. Its cooldown scales from 18 seconds to 6 seconds and is not modified by attributes, cooldown reduction, or weapon level. Its radius scales linearly from 3 m at level 1 to 12 m at level 99. Hero casts affect the hero. Creep casts affect the caster and all allied active creeps within that radius, with green floating healing text on every creep whose health is restored. The cast effect lasts exactly one second and is centered on the caster at that same effective radius: a horizontal green ground aura pulses from 0 opacity to 1 at 25% progress, then fades to 0; six separate green plus-sign sprites distributed across the aura appear at 25% progress, always face the camera, rise vertically along world Z without sliding across the ground, and fade to 0 by the effect's removal. Its spell description explicitly identifies Rage as the bonus-healing stat.

**Unique variant (Unique mace):** each time the caster's Healing cast restores HP, every enemy within the effective healing radius takes magic damage equal to 25% of the HP actually restored to the caster, applied once per cast.
