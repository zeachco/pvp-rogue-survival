# Healing (`healing`)

**Source:** globally bound to new heroes at level 1; Rare and Epic maces can add or upgrade it. Sent creeps may gain it from an equipped item or authored universal skill. **Activation:** automatic mana skill only below 75% HP. **Effect:** restores its level-scaled 20%–90% fraction of current HP, plus a flat 5% of maximum HP and a further 5% of maximum HP scaled by the caster's current Rage fraction (`currentRage / maxRage`); restoration is capped by missing HP. Each cast costs `5 + 2 * effective skill level + 0.25 * HP actually restored` mana. For a creep's area cast, the variable component is based on HP restored to the caster; healing nearby allies does not add to its cost. Its cooldown scales from 18 seconds to 6 seconds and is not modified by attributes, cooldown reduction, or weapon level. Its radius scales linearly from 3 m at level 1 to 12 m at level 99. Hero casts affect the hero. Creep casts affect the caster and all allied active creeps within the effective radius, with green floating healing text on every creep whose health is restored. The cast effect lasts exactly one second and is centered on the caster at that same effective radius: a green light aura pulses from 0 opacity to 1 at 25% progress, then fades to 0; green plus signs distributed across the aura appear at 25% progress, float upward, and fade to 0 by the effect's removal. Its spell description explicitly identifies Rage as the bonus-healing stat.
