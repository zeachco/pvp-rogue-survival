# Healing (`healing`)

**Source:** globally bound to new heroes at level 1; Rare and Epic maces can add or upgrade it. Sent creeps may gain it from equipped items or an authored universal skill. **Activation:** automatic mana skill only below 75% HP. **Effect:** restores its level-scaled 20%–90% fraction of current HP, plus a flat 5% of maximum HP and a further 5% of maximum HP scaled by the caster's current Rage fraction (`currentRage / maxRage`); restoration is capped by missing HP. Each cast costs `20 + 2 * effective skill level + 0.25 * HP actually restored` mana. For a creep's area cast, the variable component is based on HP restored to the caster; healing nearby allies does not add to its cost. Its cooldown scales from 15 seconds to 1 second and is not modified by attributes, cooldown reduction, or weapon level. Hero casts affect the hero. Creep casts affect the caster and all allied active creeps within 300 pixels, with green floating healing text and rising green mote effects on every creep whose health is restored. Its spell description explicitly identifies Rage as the bonus-healing stat.
