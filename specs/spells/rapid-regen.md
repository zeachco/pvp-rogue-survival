# Rapid Regeneration (`rapidRegen`)

**Source:** extractable from Renewal Idol relic perks. **Activation:** self-buff costing 4 mana with a 40-second base cooldown. A manual slot activation may cast at full health; automatic mode only casts while the hero is missing health. Both modes require that no Rapid Regeneration buff is already active. **Effect:** for `10 + 20 * (skillLevel - 1) / 98` seconds, multiply all normal and equipment-derived health regeneration by `3.6 + 1.4 * (skillLevel - 1) / 98`, then add a flat 0.1 HP/second. This is 360% regeneration plus 0.1 HP/second for 10 seconds at level 1, scaling linearly to the unchanged 500% cap plus 0.1 HP/second for 30 seconds at level 99. The buff never restores health above maximum. While active, Rapid Regeneration appears as a positive timed buff above the XP bar alongside XP Send and Reflective Surge.
