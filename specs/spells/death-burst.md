# Death Burst (`deathBurst`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** a nearby defeated enemy deals magic damage equal to 20% of its maximum HP to other enemies within 45% of the shared aura radius. The persistent aura under its owner is green and pointed. Each triggered burst creates an immediate red light flash, outward red particles, and a broad red ground stain centered on the defeated enemy; the particles disperse while the stain slowly fades over three seconds. The burst presentation is deterministic and does not consume combat randomness.
