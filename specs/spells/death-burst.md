# Death Burst (`deathBurst`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** a nearby defeated enemy queues a burst; on the next fixed update, it deals magic damage equal to 20% of its maximum HP to other enemies within 45% of the shared aura radius. The persistent aura under its owner is green and pointed. Each triggered burst creates an immediate red light flash and a circular ground wave that expands from the defeated enemy to the complete damage radius over 0.45 seconds, with 18 deterministic outward red particles sharing draw resources and a broad red ground stain that slowly fades over three seconds. Its fixed particle cap and persistent presentation consume neither per-frame GPU allocations nor combat randomness.
