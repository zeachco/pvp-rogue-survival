# Death Burst (`deathBurst`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** a nearby defeated enemy deals magic damage equal to 20% of its maximum HP to other enemies within 45% of the shared aura radius. The ground effect is green and pointed.
