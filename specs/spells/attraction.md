# Attraction (`attraction`)

**Source:** extractable Attraction Idol passive; some staves, relics, amulets, and charms also grant its item passive. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.001 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** uncollected item drops move directly toward the wearer at 35px/s, subject to the source item's requirement effectiveness. It does not stack, affects presentation and proximity only, and never changes server ownership or pickup authority.
