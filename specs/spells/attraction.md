# Attraction (`attraction`)

**Source:** extractable Attraction Idol passive; some staves, relics, amulets, and charms also grant its item passive. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.001 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** grants `1% * effective level` Magic Find and `1% * effective level` Gold Find. Uncollected item drops move directly toward the wearer at the source item's requirement-adjusted base speed multiplied by `1 + 3 * (effectiveLevel - 1) / 98`, scaling to four times as fast at level 99. Multiple item sources do not stack; use the fastest effective source. Attraction affects drop movement presentation and proximity only, while Magic Find and Gold Find remain server-authoritative.
