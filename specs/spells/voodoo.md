# Voodoo (`voodoo`)

**Source:** Voodoo Doll relic perk. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.005 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** poison applied by its wearer is multiplied by `1 + min(1.5, 0.03 * Spirit)`. It modifies only poison damage originating from that unit.

**Unique variant (Unique Voodoo Doll relic):** while active, the wearer also takes 20% less incoming direct damage, in addition to the normal poison amplification.
