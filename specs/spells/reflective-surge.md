# Reflective Surge (`reflectiveSurge`)

**Source:** Rare, Epic, and Unique spiked bucklers. **Activation:** 3-rage skill whose authored cooldown scales linearly from 30 seconds at level 1 to 20 seconds at level 99 before the shared attribute and equipment cooldown reductions. In auto-fire mode it activates only when its bearer is hit by a non-dodged attack or damaging effect while the skill is enabled, ready, and affordable; the triggering hit resolves against the previously compiled state and is not modified by the newly activated surge. The surge begins contributing when state is compiled on the next fixed update. It never casts merely because an enemy is in range. With auto-fire disabled, pressing the number assigned to its loadout slot activates it immediately while it is ready and affordable, with the same next-update compilation boundary. Manual and on-hit activation spend the same cost and start the same cooldown. **Effect:** for a duration scaling linearly from 5 seconds at level 1 to 19 seconds at level 99, doubles Thorns and successful-block reflection, then adds another 1% of incoming damage to every return. While active it adds 10 percentage points of block chance at level 1, scaling linearly to 30 percentage points at level 99; final block chance is capped at 95%. The affected hero or creep temporarily uses a uniform dark-gray metallic material instead of its usual textures, and the hero HUD and each creep's world resource-bar stack show a positive Reflective Surge aura icon for the effect's full active duration.
