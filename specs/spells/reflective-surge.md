# Reflective Surge (`reflectiveSurge`)

**Source:** Rare and Epic spiked bucklers. **Activation:** 3-rage skill whose authored cooldown scales linearly from 30 seconds at level 1 to 20 seconds at level 99 before the shared attribute and equipment cooldown reductions. In auto-fire mode it activates only when its bearer is hit by a non-dodged attack or damaging effect while the skill is enabled, ready, and affordable, and the triggering hit receives the skill's effect; it never casts merely because an enemy is in range. With auto-fire disabled, pressing the number assigned to its loadout slot activates it immediately while it is ready and affordable. Manual and on-hit activation spend the same cost and start the same cooldown. **Effect:** for a duration scaling linearly from 5 seconds at level 1 to 19 seconds at level 99, doubles Thorns and successful-block reflection, then adds another 1% of incoming damage to every return. While active it adds 10 percentage points of block chance at level 1, scaling linearly to 30 percentage points at level 99; final block chance is capped at 95%. The affected hero or creep temporarily uses a uniform dark-gray metallic material instead of its usual textures, and the hero HUD and each creep's world resource-bar stack show a positive Reflective Surge aura icon for the effect's full active duration.
