# Reflective Surge (`reflectiveSurge`)

**Source:** Rare and Epic spiked bucklers. **Activation:** reactive 3-rage skill with a 16-second base cooldown. It activates only when its bearer is hit by a non-dodged attack or damaging effect while the skill is enabled, ready, and affordable; it never casts merely because an enemy is in range. The triggering hit receives the skill's effect. **Effect:** for 6 seconds, doubles Thorns and successful-block reflection, then adds another 1% of incoming damage to every return.
