# Vampiric Boomerang (`vampiricBoomerang`)

**Source:** extractable charm skill. **Activation:** automatic blood skill; base cooldown 8 seconds, range 5.2 m, and 1.1 damage multiplier. It spends `max(3% of current HP, 1 HP)` after life-cost reduction, retains at least 1 HP, and adds `HP spent * (1 + skillLevel / 10)` to its base damage. **Effect:** a red 0.66 m-radius crescent travels out and back at 3.6 m/s and survives collisions. Each enemy continuously takes the Boomerang's cast damage per second for the exact time it overlaps the crescent's collision area and receives half the normal linear projectile knockback. On return it heals once for cumulative actual damage: 1% at level 1, scaling linearly to 80% at level 99.
