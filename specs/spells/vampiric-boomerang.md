# Vampiric Boomerang (`vampiricBoomerang`)

**Source:** extractable charm skill. **Activation:** automatic blood skill; base cooldown 8 seconds, range 5.2 m, and 1.1 damage multiplier. It spends `max(10% of current HP, 1 HP)` after life-cost reduction, retains at least 1 HP, and adds `HP spent * (1 + skillLevel / 10)` to its base damage. **Effect:** a red 0.66 m-radius crescent lies flat facing world-up instead of facing the camera, travels out and back at 3.6 m/s, and survives enemy collisions. Every 0.5 seconds while flying, it samples collision against every enemy and deals 0.5 seconds of its cast damage per second to each overlapping foe. Each resolved overlap receives half the normal linear projectile knockback. On return it heals once for cumulative actual damage: 1% at level 1, scaling linearly to 80% at level 99.
