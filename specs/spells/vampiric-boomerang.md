# Vampiric Boomerang (`vampiricBoomerang`)

**Source:** extractable charm skill. **Activation:** automatic blood skill; base cooldown 8 seconds, range 260px, and 1.1 damage multiplier. It spends 30% of current HP after life-cost reduction, retains at least 1 HP, and adds the actual HP spent to damage. **Effect:** a red 33px-radius crescent travels out and back at 90px/s and survives collisions. Each enemy continuously takes the Boomerang's cast damage per second for the exact time it overlaps the crescent's collision area. On return it heals once for cumulative actual damage: 1% at level 1, scaling linearly to 80% at level 99.
