# Rent (`rent`)

**Source:** universal learned skill. **Activation:** automatic blood physical attack; authored cooldown scaling linearly from 6 seconds at level 1 to 3 seconds at level 99, 3.6 m base range, and 1.25 skill multiplier. The authored cooldown is final and does not receive generic weapon-level or cooldown-reduction modifiers. It spends 10% of current HP after life-cost reduction, retaining at least 1 HP, and adds actual HP spent to damage. **Effect:** a full-circle attack that heals the caster for 1% maximum HP per distinct enemy touched. Its hero visual uses four pointed purple magic-sword cones spaced in the four cardinal directions and driving a fast clockwise slash around the caster; enemy casts are hostile red. A shader-displaced, tapered 3D ribbon trails each cone instead of a flat disc. The cones and trails orbit at elbow height: 80% of the caster model's complete presentation height above the gameplay plane.
