# Rent (`rent`)

**Source:** universal learned skill. **Activation:** automatic blood physical attack; 4-second base cooldown, 3.6 m base range, and 1.25 skill multiplier. It spends 10% of current HP after life-cost reduction, retaining at least 1 HP, and adds actual HP spent to damage. **Effect:** a full-circle attack that heals the caster for 1% maximum HP per distinct enemy touched. Its visual is three tightly spaced red, white, and red oval edge lines.
