# Rent (`rent`)

**Source:** universal learned skill. **Activation:** automatic blood physical attack; 4-second base cooldown, 3.6 m base range, and 1.25 skill multiplier. It spends 10% of current HP after life-cost reduction, retaining at least 1 HP, and adds actual HP spent to damage. **Effect:** a full-circle attack that heals the caster for 1% maximum HP per distinct enemy touched. Its hero visual is a pointed purple magic sword driving a fast clockwise slash around the caster, beginning at the caster's front-left and passing through front-right, back, and left; enemy casts are hostile red. A shader-displaced, tapered 3D ribbon trails the sword instead of a flat disc. The sword and trail orbit at elbow height: 80% of the caster model's complete presentation height above the gameplay plane.
