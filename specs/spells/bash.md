# Bash (`bash`)

**Source:** club signature skill. **Activation:** automatic rage physical attack; final cooldown scales linearly from 8 seconds at effective level 1 to 5 seconds at effective level 99, range 1.05 m, and 1.5 damage multiplier. The cooldown is not modified by Intelligence, Agility, generic cooldown reduction, or weapon level. **Effect:** a full-circle hit that always stuns a hit target for 1.1 seconds. It uses the shared physical-hit, rage-cost, pushback, dodge, mitigation, effective-level, and weapon-level rules for its other properties.

**Unique variant (Unique club):** the Bash full-circle hit also destroys every enemy projectile within its effective range at the moment of impact, removing it without dealing its damage or effects.
