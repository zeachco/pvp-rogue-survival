# Rending Throw (`rendingThrow`)

**Source:** throwing-axe signature skill. **Activation:** automatic rage projectile; base cooldown 4 seconds, range 4.8 m, and 0.45 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a short-ranged physical projectile that applies its standard linear projectile force and guarantees an 18-second, 0.25-damage-per-second bleed on every hit, in addition to independently rolled weapon effects. At level 1 it hits one target with no pierce. From level 2 onward, its pierce count is `1 + floor((skillLevel - 2) / 3)`, and its total target limit is `1 + pierce`: level 2 pierces one target, with one additional pierce at levels 5, 8, 11, and every third level thereafter. The projectile remains active through targets until it reaches that computed limit.

**Presentation:** the hero projectile is a visibly enlarged throwing axe with purple-edged blades and a translucent purple aura; enemy casts are hostile red. The complete camera-facing presentation spins rapidly throughout flight while the aura pulses, distinguishing Rending Throw from an ordinary throwing-axe attack.
