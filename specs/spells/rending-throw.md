# Rending Throw (`rendingThrow`)

**Source:** throwing-axe signature skill. **Activation:** automatic stamina projectile; base cooldown 4 seconds, range 240px, and 1.35 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a short-ranged physical projectile that applies its standard linear projectile force and guarantees the standard 3-second, 0.25-damage-per-second bleed on hit, in addition to independently rolled weapon effects.
