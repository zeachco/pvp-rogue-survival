# Rending Throw (`rendingThrow`)

**Source:** throwing-axe signature skill. **Activation:** automatic rage projectile; base cooldown 4 seconds, range 4.8 m, and 1.35 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a short-ranged physical projectile that applies its standard linear projectile force and guarantees the standard 9-second, 0.25-damage-per-second bleed on hit, in addition to independently rolled weapon effects.

**Presentation:** the projectile is a visibly enlarged throwing axe with crimson-edged blades and a translucent blood-red aura. The complete camera-facing presentation spins rapidly throughout flight while the aura pulses, distinguishing Rending Throw from an ordinary throwing-axe attack.
