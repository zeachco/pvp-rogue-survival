# Hindering Aura (`hinderingAura`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers. **Activation:** zero-cost togglable passive. **Effect:** nearby active enemies have attack speed multiplied from 0.8 at level 1 to 0.5 at level 99. It uses the shared aura radius and blue floor ripples, can coexist with other auras, and refreshes each simulation update rather than creating timed status instances.
