# Blocking (`blocking`)

**Source:** extractable buckler passive. **Activation:** reactive while enabled, with Rage upkeep of `0.001 * effective level` per second and no reactive cooldown; successful blocks additionally spend any equipped buckler's calculated rage cost. **Effect:** each effective Blocking level adds one half percentage point of base block chance even without an equipped buckler, independently of buckler requirement effectiveness. An equipped buckler adds its own attribute-scaled block chance. The character sheet's Block chance row includes the passive contribution in both cases. Every blockable damage event independently rolls block chance, and a successful block prevents `min(incomingDamage, Strength)` under the shared resource and mitigation rules. Final block chance is capped at 90%, including while Reflective Surge is active.
