# Arcane Bolt (`arcaneBolt`)

**Source:** staff signature skill. **Activation:** automatic magic projectile; base cooldown 5 seconds, range 6.6 m, and 1.7 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a long-range projectile that applies its standard linear projectile force and the standard 4-second Freeze on a successful direct hit. Freeze cancels self-propelled motion, but the Bolt's impact force and every later impact continue to slide the frozen target without friction.
