# Arcane Bolt (`arcaneBolt`)

**Source:** staff signature skill. **Activation:** automatic magic projectile; base cooldown 5 seconds, range 6.6 m, and 1.7 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a long-range projectile that applies its standard linear projectile force and explodes on impact. The explosion radius scales linearly from 1 meter at effective skill level 1 to 4 meters at level 99, and every enemy in the blast takes `0.5 * spellPower(level)` additional magic damage. The impact creates a short-lived expanding arcane burst and a bright blue light that fades with the explosion.

**Unique variant (Unique staff):** the explosion radius is doubled, and every enemy in the blast receives a 4-second Freeze. Unique adds no separate explosion damage beyond the standard Arcane Bolt explosion.
