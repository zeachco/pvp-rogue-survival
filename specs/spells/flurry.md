# Flurry (`flurry`)

**Source:** dagger signature skill. **Activation:** automatic stamina physical attack; base cooldown scales linearly from 10 seconds at level 1 to 1 second at level 99 before Intelligence/Agility and weapon-level cooldown modifiers, range 105px, and 0.8 damage multiplier. **Effect:** a wide close-range arc with recovery `0.35 / attacksPerSecond`, rather than ordinary recovery. It otherwise uses the shared physical-hit rules.
