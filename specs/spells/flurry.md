# Flurry (`flurry`)

**Source:** dagger signature skill. **Activation:** automatic rage physical attack; effective cooldown scales linearly from 8 seconds at effective level 1 to 5 seconds at effective level 99, range is 1.05 m, authored sector is 63 degrees, and damage multiplier is 0.8. This authored cooldown is final and is not further reduced by Intelligence, Agility, generic cooldown reduction, or weapon level. **Effect:** a focused close-range arc with recovery `0.35 / attacksPerSecond`, rather than ordinary recovery. It otherwise uses the shared physical-hit rules.
