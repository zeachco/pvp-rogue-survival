# Fire Breath (`fireBreath`)

**Source:** Ember Idol relic perk; it is enemy-eligible. **Activation:** automatic 4-mana magic skill; 9-second base cooldown, 3 m base range, and 1.1 damage multiplier. **Effect:** advancing fire arcs form a forward cone and apply an eight-second Burn that ticks for `0.25 + 0.03 * Spirit` damage without applying force.
