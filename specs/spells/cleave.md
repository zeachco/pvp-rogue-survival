# Cleave (`cleave`)

**Source:** axe signature skill. **Activation:** automatic rage physical attack with a 0.3625 damage multiplier and an effective cooldown that scales linearly from 6 seconds at effective level 1 to 3 seconds at effective level 99. This authored cooldown is final and is not further reduced by Intelligence, Agility, generic cooldown reduction, or weapon level. Its attack sector scales linearly from 45 degrees to 270 degrees across those same levels. Its base range independently scales linearly from 1 m to 10 m before shared Spirit and weapon-level modifiers. **Effect:** a broad physical axe strike with twice the standard radial knockback that applies a 6-second bleed dealing 0.45 damage per second. The player-facing description states the sector and range scaling plus the stronger knockback.

**Unique variant (Unique axe):** the Cleave strike also destroys every enemy projectile whose center lies within its attack sector and effective range at the moment of impact, removing it without dealing its damage or effects.
