# Time Harvest (`timeHarvest`)

**Source:** random Amulet roll; extractable passive. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.002 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** after the wearer defeats an enemy, remove 1 second from every hero cooldown at level 1. The reduction scales linearly with effective skill level and caps at 10 seconds at level 99. An equipped amulet grants a temporary Time Harvest level that scales from 1 at item level 0 to 99 at item level 50; extracted permanent levels use normal skill progression. It applies to basic attacks, active spells, Healing, Blocking, and aura pulses.
