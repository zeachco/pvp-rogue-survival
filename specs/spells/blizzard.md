# Blizzard (`blizzard`)

**Source:** Rare, Epic, and Unique staves. **Availability:** immediate once learned or supplied by equipment. **Activation:** automatic Mana skill with a 20-second base cooldown; it targets the closest enemy within the staff's spell range and fixes the storm at that enemy's cast-time position. **Effect:** icicles rain on the fixed target position for a level-scaled duration. Each icicle visibly falls, then deals Cold area damage and applies one 4-second Frost stack to every enemy in its impact area when it touches the ground. The impact area is 2 m at level 1 and 4 m at level 99. Duration scales from 5 to 15 seconds, and emission rate scales from 1 to 3 icicles per second. Each cast emits immediately and then at the level-scaled interval while the duration remains.

All values interpolate linearly from effective level 1 through 99. Mana cost scales from 55 to 300; its level-1 cost equals maximum Mana at 25 Intelligence (`5 + 2 * 25`), replacing its former hero-level-25 requirement. Each icicle deals `skill level + 1.2 * Intelligence` damage, producing `1 + 1.2 * Intelligence` at level 1 and `99 + 1.2 * Intelligence` at level 99. Blizzard damage is spell damage, may critically strike using the cast's single rolled critical result, and has no impact force. Blizzard's own radius is not increased by Spirit; Spirit still increases its targeting range through the shared spell-range rule.
