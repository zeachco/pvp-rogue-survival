# Orbiting Hammers (`orbitingHammers`)

**Source:** hammer signature skill. **Availability:** immediate once learned or supplied by equipment. **Activation:** automatic 25-Mana magic skill; base cooldown 12 seconds, range 4.8 m, and 0.85 damage multiplier. Its cost equals maximum Mana at 10 Intelligence (`5 + 2 * 10`), replacing its former hero-level-10 requirement. **Effect:** launches three hammers at evenly spaced angles around the fixed world point where the spell was cast; they do not follow later caster movement. They orbit at 5.2 radians/s with deterministic drift, expand from a 0.56 m to 3.8 m radius over their first 2.4 seconds, then orbit at that radius. The spell owns one warm-gold point light, carried by only one of the three hammers. Their compact low-poly 3D models visibly tumble around all three local axes throughout flight; a camera-facing hammer silhouette is used while the model loads or if loading fails. Their lifetime scales linearly as `2.4 + 7.6 * (skillLevel - 1) / 98` seconds, from 2.4 seconds at level 1 to 10 seconds at level 99; they survive hits and may damage each enemy once.

**Unique variant (Unique hammer):** the hammers orbit the caster and follow its position for the full active duration instead of orbiting the fixed world point where the spell was cast. Orbit speed, radius expansion, lifetime, and damage are otherwise unchanged.
