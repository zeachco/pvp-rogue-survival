# Orbiting Hammers (`orbitingHammers`)

**Source:** hammer signature skill. **Availability:** requires hero level 10. It remains visible as locked in the full spell catalog before then and is hidden by Learned only until eligible. **Activation:** automatic 6-mana magic skill; base cooldown 12 seconds, range 4.8 m, and 0.85 damage multiplier. **Effect:** launches three hammers at evenly spaced angles around the fixed world point where the spell was cast; they do not follow later caster movement. They orbit at 5.2 radians/s with deterministic drift, expand from a 0.56 m to 3.8 m radius over their first 2.4 seconds, then orbit at that radius. Their compact low-poly 3D models visibly tumble around all three local axes throughout flight; a camera-facing hammer silhouette is used while the model loads or if loading fails. Their lifetime scales linearly as `2.4 + 7.6 * (skillLevel - 1) / 98` seconds, from 2.4 seconds at level 1 to 10 seconds at level 99; they survive hits and may damage each enemy once.

**Unique variant (Unique hammer):** the hammers orbit the caster and follow its position for the full active duration instead of orbiting the fixed world point where the spell was cast. Orbit speed, radius expansion, lifetime, and damage are otherwise unchanged.
