# Thunder Aura (`thunderAura`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers; Scepters carry one deterministic aura. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** within the shared aura radius, it strikes one random enemy every 10 seconds at level 1 down to every 3 seconds at level 99 for `3 + 0.35 * Magic` electric damage. It has `10% + normal critical chance`; a critical chains once for 60% damage. The field is translucent white electric energy.
