# Thunder Aura (`thunderAura`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers; Scepters carry one deterministic aura. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.01 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** within twice the shared aura radius, it strikes one random enemy every 10 seconds at level 1 down to every 3 seconds at level 99 for `6 + 0.7 * Intelligence` electric damage. It has `10% + normal critical chance`; a critical chains once for 60% damage. Thunder Aura has no persistent filled field. Each primary or chained impact draws electric arcs inward from the impact area's edges to the struck enemy and uses one renderer-pooled light very close above the ground; the light and arcs fade over 1.5 seconds.
