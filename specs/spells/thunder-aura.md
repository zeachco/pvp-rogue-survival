# Thunder Aura (`thunderAura`)

**Source:** extractable aura on Scepters and qualifying Holy Bucklers; Scepters carry one deterministic aura. **Activation:** zero-cost togglable passive. **Effect:** within the shared aura radius, it strikes one random enemy every 10 seconds at level 1 down to every 1 second at level 99 for `3 + 0.6 * Intelligence` electric damage. It has `10% + normal critical chance`; a critical chains once for 60% damage. The field is translucent white electric energy.
