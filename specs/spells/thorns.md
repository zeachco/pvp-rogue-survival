# Thorns (`thorns`)

**Source:** Rare and Epic spiked bucklers. **Activation:** togglable passive with Mana upkeep of `0.005 * effective level` per second. **Effect:** returns 5% of incoming direct damage even when no block succeeds. Returned damage follows the shared reflection rules and is doubled during Reflective Surge. Thorns deliberately creates no projectile or standalone spell animation: the damaged creep uses the shared one-render-frame white hit flash, making otherwise indirect return damage visible when no other hit presentation overlaps it.
